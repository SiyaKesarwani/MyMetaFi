MyMetaFi is a dapp where one can co-own their favourite NFTs with the people around the world. This is done
through a process of crowdfunding the value of NFT together. Typically, this NFT is then fractionalized and
ownership is split based on the contribution amount.
During their ownership they can also get the leverage of renting, licensing the NFT on different supported
platforms and divide the earnings among themselves. After the NFT is sold to supported NFT marketplaces, the
owners get their shares accordingly.
